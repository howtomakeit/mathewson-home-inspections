What we inspect

A thorough look at the systems that protect your home.

From the roofline to the foundation, your inspection should leave you with a clear understanding of the property and the next decisions in front of you.

01

Whole-home inspections

A visual review of the home’s accessible structural, exterior, roofing, plumbing, electrical, heating, cooling, interior, insulation and appliance systems.

02

Buyer & seller inspections

Know what you are buying—or identify issues before listing—so you can plan repairs, negotiations and maintenance with better information.
